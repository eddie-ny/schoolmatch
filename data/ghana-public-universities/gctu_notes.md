# GCTU (Ghana Communication Technology University): collected 2026-10-14

Captured 26 of 26 degree programmes listed at https://site.gctu.edu.gh/undergraduate-programmes (titles from there).

## Sources
- Programme list: https://site.gctu.edu.gh/undergraduate-programmes (no per-programme requirements or cut-offs).
- 2026/2027 undergraduate admissions: https://site.gctu.edu.gh/?p=23902 (says all degree programmes are 4 years; lists 22 programmes with slightly different names, for example "Accounting with Computing" versus "Accounting Information Technology", Banking and Finance as an option of Business Administration).
- Entry requirements: https://site.gctu.edu.gh/undergraduate-admission-requirement

## Rule
Credit passes in English Language, Core Mathematics, Integrated Science (science programmes) or Social Studies (non-science) plus three relevant electives; aggregate 24 (SSSCE) or 36 (WASSCE) or better. Entry maximum, not stored as a cut-off. Department-specific requirements apply but are not published. No cut-off list published.
