# UEW masters notes

Rows: 109, all unverified (verified column blank). Collected 2026-10-18.

Source: https://www.uew.edu.gh/sites/default/files/2025-05/postgraduate_flyer_2025.pdf (UEW postgraduate flyer, 2025). It was read through a summariser, so wording is approximate. Also checked https://uew.edu.gh/node/2307 (Special Education pages only; titles, no requirements).

Rules as printed:
- 2-year MPhil (regular) and weekend MBA: "a good first degree in the relevant field of study". IFAC-qualified accountants may also apply.
- One-year top-up to MPhil: a good MEd, MA or MSc in the relevant field. Stored as "(1-year top-up)" in the name, duration 1.
- MEd (sandwich and distance): good bachelor's with an education component; applicants without one need PGDE or Diploma in Education.
- MA/MSc (sandwich and weekend): good first degree in the relevant field.
- All applicants: selection interview. Research degrees: research proposal. Foreign qualifications: GTEC evaluation.

Gaps and cautions:
- No degree class is printed anywhere, so min_degree_class is blank for every row. "Good" is not a class.
- The MPhil requirement line on the flyer is garbled in the extraction (it seems to mention "at least grade..."). Check the PDF by eye before trusting the blank.
- MFA Theatre Arts and MA Theatre Arts sit under the regular MPhil list with no requirement of their own; the general MPhil rule is noted.
- Executive Masters in Human Rights, Conflict and Peace Studies: no requirement stated.
- Duplicates across faculties (for example Applied Linguistics) were collapsed to one row. MEd programmes offered in both distance and sandwich are one row.
- PhD, EdD and PGDTLHE (a diploma) were left out. Fees are not in the source.
