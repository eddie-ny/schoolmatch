# Accra Technical University notes

Collected 2026-10-19 (technical universities started).

Source (official): https://atu.edu.gh/gradsch/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ATU-ADMISSION-2026-2027-ACADEMIC-YEAR.pdf , the 2026/2027 admission notice (closing date 30 September 2026), read through a summarising tool, so spot check it. Site: https://atu.edu.gh. Technical universities list from Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_universities_in_Ghana), not an official list: Accra, Bolgatanga, Cape Coast, Kumasi, Koforidua, Tamale, Ho, Takoradi, Sunyani and Wa.

Loaded: 33 four-year BTech/BSc programmes (step 45) plus 1 Evening row for BTech Medical Laboratory Science (the notice says Morning/Evening). General rule for all: credit passes A1 to C6 in English, Core Mathematics and Integrated Science plus three relevant electives, cumulative aggregate 36 or better (24 or better for SSSCE). The 36 is stated as the entry limit, so it is stored as cut-off 36 with a note saying it is a stated maximum, not a past cut-off. Core Mathematics C6 and English Language C6 are stored as requirements. Departmental requirements also apply and are not published in the notice.

Not loaded: 2-year top-up BTech programmes (18 listed, weekend or evening), HND programmes (18), diplomas, masters, mature entry, and the two special ATU and NUIST (China) programmes (two years at ATU, then two years in China, minimum B3 in Elective Mathematics). No tuition fees are in the notice, only the GHS 230 application voucher. No campus is stated per programme, and no programme is called fee-paying.
