# Bolgatanga Technical University notes

Collected 2026-10-20. Sources (official, both undated): programmes https://bolgatu.edu.gh/under-graduate-programmes and entry rules https://bolgatu.edu.gh/?p=11863 (the entry page cites an application deadline of 29 September 2023 but shows a 2025 copyright). Read through a summarising tool. 18 four-year BSc and BTech programmes loaded (step 51), all marked unverified because of the missing dates. The programmes page says full-time at the Sumbrungu Campus. BTech Industrial Art is one row (options Graphic Communication Design, Fashion Design and Textiles, Sculpture, Ceramics, Painting and Decorating).

Entry rule: six credit passes (A1 to C6 for WASSCE) including English Language, Mathematics and Social Studies or Integrated Science, plus at least three relevant electives. Core Mathematics C6 and English Language C6 stored as requirements. No aggregate or cut-off or fees published.

Not loaded: weekend top-up programmes (17, Sumbrungu Campus), HND (17 full-time, 4 evening at the Bukere Campus), diplomas, pre-engineering and access courses. The evening HND at Bukere Campus and the weekend top-ups are not degree entry, so no campus rows were made.
