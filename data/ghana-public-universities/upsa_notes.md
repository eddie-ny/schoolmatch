
## 2026-10-18 cut-offs
- 13 programmes got a 2026 cut-off from https://yen.com.gh/education/311656-upsa-releases-2026-cut-points-59-undergraduate-diploma-programmes/ (news report of UPSA's announcement, 10 Sep 2026). Those rows were set unverified.
- The report names 47 of 59 programmes. Applied Statistics, Agribusiness and Finance, Applied French and Communications and Communication Studies are not named in it, so they stay blank. Report programmes not in our database (Auditing, Cyber Security, Software Engineering and others) were not added as new programmes.
- Matching was by name: the report says "BSc Real Estate Management" and "BSc Public Relations Management" while we store "Real Estate Management and Finance" and "BA Public Relations Management", so those two are less certain.
- UPSA's own page (https://admissions.upsa.edu.gh/?p=7485) lists programmes but no cut-offs.
