Rules: one set for MSc and MPhil, a good first degree in a relevant field with at least Second Class Lower, an official transcript, a concept paper and departmental requirements. The handbook adds that Third Class may be considered with work experience, an interview panel recommendation or an entrance exam.

Blank class: all one-year top-up rows (for holders of a non-research masters), both MEd rows, and Master of Public Health, because the notice states no separate requirement. The handbook says MEd needs a good B.Ed. in a related field.
