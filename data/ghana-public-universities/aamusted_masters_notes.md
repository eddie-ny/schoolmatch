Rules as stored:
- MPhil (2 years): B.Sc., B.Ed., B.A., B.Tech or equivalent with at least Second Class Lower in the relevant area; 36 credit hours; competitive exam or interview; two academic references.
- MTech, MSc, MEd, MA (1 year): same class rule. The 2024 requirements also allow a Third Class or Pass with three years of work experience after an entrance exam.
- MBA: good first degree, preferably Second Class Lower or better (or GPA 2.0); Third Class may be considered with work experience.
- One-year top-ups: need an MTech, MA, MSc, MEd, MBA or equivalent; no class stated, so the class is blank.
- Master of Public Health: no programme-specific requirement printed; class blank.

Cautions:
- The five Mampong MEd programmes (Agriculture options and Science Education) have no requirement of their own in the advert, so the general one-year rule is applied.
- Crop Sciences and Animal Science are one row each with their specialisations in the title, as printed.
- The "(Top-Up)" rows for MPhil Educational Leadership and English have no mode stated in the advert.
- Fees not read. PhD and diplomas left out.
