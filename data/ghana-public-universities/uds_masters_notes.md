Class rules as stored:

- Second Class Upper: MPhil Public Health Nutrition, MSc/MPhil Biotechnology (direct entry; Second Class Lower considered after interview), MPhil Integrated Water Resource Management and Irrigation and Drainage Engineering (Second Class Lower may be considered), MSc Community Disaster Resilience Studies (or 10 years' service).
- Blank: no class printed, "good first degree" only, or "First or Second Class Honours" without Upper or Lower (Environmental Management and Sustainability, Agroforestry and Dryland Conservation).
