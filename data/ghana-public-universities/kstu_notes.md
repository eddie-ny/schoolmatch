# Kumasi Technical University notes

Collected 2026-10-20.

Sources:
- Programme list (official, current): https://kstu.edu.gh/academics/undergraduate/programmes , read through a summarising tool. 46 four-year undergraduate programmes loaded (step 46), titles shortened to BEng, BTech, BSc and BA. The page lists BEng and BA degrees as well as BTech and BSc, and a few near duplicates (BEng and BTech Automotive Engineering, BEng and BTech Civil Engineering, BEng and BTech Electrical and Electronic Engineering, BTech and BSc Agribusiness with Entrepreneurship), kept as listed.
- Campus (official notice, no year): https://kstu.edu.gh/node/1171 says students in the faculties of Built and Natural Environment, Creative Arts and Technology, and Entrepreneurship and Enterprise Development attend lectures at the Adako-Jachie Campus, not the Asem main campus. Campus set for BTech Interior Design Technology, BSc Facilities Management, BSc Estate Management, BTech Building Technology, BTech Fashion Design and Textiles Studies and BTech Agribusiness with Entrepreneurship. The notice mentions a "BTech Entrepreneurship and Finance"; the programmes page lists "BSc Entrepreneurship and Finance" under the Business School, so no campus was set for it.
- Entry rule: https://archive.kstu.edu.gh/sites/default/files/announcement_files/ADMISSION%20advert.pdf , the 2022/2023 admission notice (old). Credit passes A1 to C6 in English Language, Core Mathematics and Integrated Science plus credits in three electives. Because it is an older notice, all rows are marked unverified and the entry note says so. Core Mathematics C6 and English Language C6 stored as requirements.

No aggregate or cut-off is published for undergraduate programmes (the only limits, 36 WASSCE and 24 SSSCE, are for a Mass Communication diploma), so cut-offs are blank. No tuition fees found; a 2026/2027 provisional fees page is linked from the site but was not opened. The 2022/2023 notice shows Regular, Evening and Weekend options for some programmes, and BTech top-up programmes for HND holders; none were loaded because no current list was found. HND, top-up (BTech Estate Management Top Up), diploma and masters programmes not loaded.
