# UENR notes

## 2026-10-19 undergraduate requirements loaded
Source (official): https://uenr.edu.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026_2027-Academic-Year-Admission-Requirements-for-Diploma-and-Undergratduate-Programmes-REVISED-EDITION-1.pdf (2026/2027 requirements, revised edition 1, read through a summarising tool). It gives the general rule (credit passes A1 to C6 in six subjects, three core and three relevant electives) and per programme elective rules, but no aggregate, no cut-offs, no modes and no fees; the only fee is the GHS 230 application e-voucher. Weekend programmes exist (contact number on the page) but none are listed. 39 existing UG rows got an entry note with the elective rule. Campus is stated only for Agriculture and Technology and Geosciences programmes, at Dormaa-Ahenkro, and that is in the entry note. BSc Medical Laboratory Science and BSc Nursing need an entrance exam and a selection interview. BSc Engineering Physics is in the PDF but not in the catalogue. UENR UG cut-offs are still blank. Closing date on the document: 6 November 2026.
