# Nursing, midwifery and health training colleges: research notes (nothing loaded)

Checked 2026-10-20. Decisions from the client: government colleges only, a new Diploma level, and no aggregate unless a current official source is found.

What was found:
- Institution list: https://www.knust.edu.gh/sites/default/files/2026-02/MINISTRY%20OF%20HEALTH%20TRAINING%20INSTITUTIONS%20AFFILIATED%20TO%20KNUST.pdf names 77 Ministry of Health training institutions affiliated to KNUST, with town only. It does not say which are government and which are mission colleges, and gives no programmes, durations or quotas. Mission names are visible (Presbyterian, SDA, Holy Family, St. Patrick's, St. John of God and others), so a government-only list cannot be built from it alone.
- Wikipedia list (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nursing_Training_Colleges_in_Ghana): 63 colleges, mixes public and private, no ownership column.
- Entry rule, 2016 only: https://nmc.gov.gh/web/phocadownloadpap/ENTRY%20REQUIREMENT2016.pdf , culled from the Daily Graphic of 7 January 2016. Basic programmes: WASSCE aggregate 30 or better in six subjects (24 for SSSCE), age 18 to 35, credits A1 to C6 in English, Mathematics and Integrated Science. Auxiliary programmes: aggregate 48 (30 for SSSCE), passes A1 to E8. Not loaded because it is ten years old.
- Programme types, unofficial: https://unicheckgh.com/universities/public-nursing-training-colleges/programs lists 41 programme types (15 degree such as BSc Public Health Nursing, 7 certificate, 19 diploma such as Registered General Nursing, Registered Midwifery, Registered Mental Health Nursing, Public Health Nursing) with an average cut-off of 36, no per-programme figures and no college names. Unverified.
- Berekum college page (https://nmtcberekum.edu.gh/index.php/academics/accreditation): Registered General Nursing diploma and Post Basic Midwifery, affiliated to KNUST and UCC. Ownership not stated.

What could not be reached: the Ministry of Health admissions site (healthtraining.gov.gh) is closed to the fetch tool (robots), and individual college sites were not reachable without a link from search. Current official entry requirements, quotas and per-college programme lists were therefore not obtained.
